Privacy Policy

This page explains how Bridlington Gold Radio handles your information when you use our app or listen online.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly through this app. Our streaming provider may collect basic technical data such as your IP address and device type for the purpose of delivering the audio stream and monitoring performance.

Cookies and analytics

This app does not set its own tracking cookies. If you access external links (for example, our website or social media), those services may use their own cookies and analytics tools.
